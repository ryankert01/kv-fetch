The idea in one picture

Keep the right KV pages close, and fetch them early

The model's memory of the prompt (the KV cache) is split into pages of 16 tokens. When the GPU can't hold them all, each page lives in one of three tiers:

Every decode step, attention only needs a few pages. The project's bet is that we can see a page's importance trending up and fetch it over PCIe before it's needed. That way the GPU never waits. The gap between "fetched" and "needed" is the lead time:

Illustration only. A page's importance rises and crosses the "important" cut-off at step 22. A naive predictor that copies the previous step notices one step late, so the GPU stalls. A predictor that extrapolates the trend fetches it 4 steps early, and the transfer is hidden.

§3 · Is the attention recorder correct?

Yes: errors are at most 0.12× the allowed floating-point noise

The recorder re-computes attention and compares itself, in the same forward pass, with the library's own reference implementation. The bar is the worst error divided by what float32 rounding allows. Anything under 1.0 means "identical up to rounding".

The first page (an "attention sink") and the pages holding freshly generated tokens are always kept on the GPU. Their share of all attention:

What this means. So accuracy metrics are computed only over the pages that actually need choosing. Otherwise every predictor would look excellent just by keeping the first page. The share differs a lot between model families, which matters when comparing a small and a reference model.

Input for one of your decisions

How much lead time does the hardware actually need?

For Llama-3.1-8B, one page is 2 MiB across all layers. At ~24 GB/s PCIe and a ~20 ms decode step, one step can move about 229 pages. The GPU keeps 410 pages attended (5 % of a 128K context):

56 % of the attended set can be replaced in a single step.

What this means. So with one request at a time, being even one step early is usually enough. Extra lead time only matters for big bursts of new pages, for many requests sharing the link, or for slower links. Choose LEAD_TIME_MIN for the regime the paper targets.

What I need from you before Phase 1

Four decisions

1The go/no-go thresholds (GRADUAL_SHARE_MIN, LEAD_TIME_MIN). If gradual rises are too rare or lead times too short, the trend idea gets demoted to a helper. Please also pick what "share" is measured over.
Suggestion: measure the share over "pages that become important after being unimportant", since those are exactly the ones that could stall the GPU. Set LEAD_TIME_MIN from the hardware box above.
2The custom multi-turn task: documents, number of turns, how later questions refer back, and how answers are scored.
Suggestion: feed each new question as generated steps. That also fixes Surprise 1, because the jump then happens while we are recording.
3Full recordings or top-512.
Recommendation: full, with fewer layers or prompts (§2, §7).
4Which models.
Recommendation: Qwen3-4B for fast iteration at 32K, with Qwen3-8B as the reference (same family and same attention-head layout). Catch: 128K needs a context-extension setting (YaRN) and its own long-context test. Alternative: the Llama pair has a natively-128K reference, but its small model only passes at 16K.
